# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 6, 2026_

Lesson Manager is a personal portfolio project by Ofek Vardimon. This page explains
what happens to your information when you sign in.

## What is collected

- **Your email address and name**, from Google sign-in or the email you sign up with.
  They are used only to create your account and show who is signed in.
- **What you enter in the app**: schools, groups, lessons, attendance, tasks and
  notes. This is stored so the app can show it back to you.

Nothing else is collected. There is no tracking, advertising or analytics.

## Where it is stored

Data is stored in [Supabase](https://supabase.com) (hosted in the EU). Row Level
Security means each account can only read and change its own data.

## Sharing

Your information is never sold or shared with anyone.

## Deleting your data

Email xeleton@gmail.com and your account and all of its data will be deleted.
